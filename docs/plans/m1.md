## 4. Ground rules
- Run from a Claude Code session in C:\Projects\Optilux on branch m1; prompts from docs/prompts/m1.md verbatim; no questions mid-run except the named stops: the approval of this plan, and 0.01.10's attended steps (the /mcp reconnect and the look review).
- Every game launch is announced before it starts with its tier, world and purpose (AGENTS.md Rules). runtime/ is written only by `install`, `launch` and the game; the user's played instance is never touched; no system settings (git config `--local` only; Gradle writes under mod/.gradle and the user's Gradle home; no JDK download by Gradle: the Temurin under runtime/java/ is its JDK).
- Commits under the user's name, no attribution, one per phase, pushed right after; never force, never `--no-verify`, never to main.
- Every phase ends test-green: `optilux test`, ruff, `optilux verify docs`, and from 0.01.04 `optilux mod test`. A failing check is fixed inside the phase, never skipped. No test in CI launches the game or runs Gradle (D19).
- Docs: a phase that changes behaviour a doc describes fixes the doc in the same commit (P36); mod.md and mod-protocol.md leave "rough draft" in their Status lines as the phases build them; byte caps measured after every edit (platform.md has 190 bytes: a sentence added there needs a cut); LF without BOM; AGENTS.md Layout gains mod/, snapshots/ and results/ in the phase that creates each; Status lines of AGENTS.md and roadmap.md updated in every phase commit.
- Identity may still move in M1 (the launch spec in 0.01.02, suite.json display in 0.01.03, the mod jar at every build): nothing is calibrated yet, and every change is committed in its phase so M2 calibrates on a fixed set.
- Complementary's source never goes to a third-party model: a /critique `--repo` run works on a copy without reference/, runtime/, snapshots/ and results/raw/.
- Time per phase is recorded in the handoff against its estimate; a stop overwrites docs/handoff.md and reports.

## 7. Stop conditions
Stop, record in docs/handoff.md and report; never work around:
- `install` finds a file whose hash differs from its pin, or a spec difference beyond P6's (the user re-pins);
- the spike's world is absent (D23);
- a launch fails to join twice in a row, the game crashes, or the Iris modal dialog appears;
- the named pipe fails its acceptance after one fix attempt (hello never answers, the DACL cannot be set, the client cannot connect): E1 is the user's decision (design.md#8-open-decisions);
- a target of platform.md#mod-adapter-surface is absent from the pinned jar and one read finds no equivalent;
- A2 differs on a view after one retake (lessons.md#determinism: keep the frame, retake once), or A9 exceeds 1 ms on the stamp D26 chooses, or A7's positive control moves nothing;
- a phase's tests cannot be made green within twice its estimate;
- a doc cap would be exceeded and the cut needs a decision;
- CI stays red for a cause outside the repository for more than 30 min;
- anything would need a system setting, elevation, a system-wide install, or a JDK download by Gradle.
