## 1. Goal and exit
- Goal: Optilux's base pack draws every program Iris draws with its own deferred lighting, Low shadows, sky and tonemap; function tests and frame captures check it before a measured session, its cost is measured on every view against its options and against Unbound, and E2 is decided.
- Exit (design.md#6-milestones M4; roadmap.md#m1-to-m6), all of:
  - `check L3`, `check L4`, `run`'s capture kind and measured runs of the Optilux pack run, tests green;
  - the M4 pack passes `check L0`, its determinism rule included, `check L3` and `verify similarity`; every coverage-list program written, each with its coverage views or a waiver in config/pipeline.json v1, shader.md#coverage-list filled;
  - two visual sessions of the M4 pack on the 11 perf views identical on every view;
  - config/calibrations/ holds quick and full of M4's identity, per mode the twin inside its threshold and shadows off beyond it;
  - full-mode cost rows on every perf view: BENCH_DETERMINISTIC on against off, each M4 option against its off; the default faster than Unbound played on every view (D84);
  - E2 decided in design.md#8-open-decisions; E3's evidence recorded (D82);
  - CI green on every push and on the PR; the user's rebase merge and release v0.5.0; docs/handoff.md written.

## 7. Stop conditions
Stop, record in docs/handoff.md and report; never work around:
- a file off its pin, or a launch spec that differs (the user re-pins);
- a launch that fails to join twice in a row, a crash, or an Iris dialog;
- the M4 pack failing to load (a compile error or a fallback) after one fix; a probe (P23) failing a decision's path and its fallback both;
- a span from reference/ that a rewrite cannot clear;
- a perf view not identical across two visual sessions, after one fix;
- the default not faster than Unbound played on a perf view (D84);
- in a calibration: shadows off not detected, or the twin beyond the threshold in two runs of a mode; a geo-mean threshold above 2 %; two sessions in a row invalid on focus, or D42 refusing twice; a fault in a measured module (a fix voids the sessions run);
- after the freeze, any change identity would see;
- a phase's tests not green within twice its estimate; a cap cut that needs a decision; CI red for a cause outside the repository for more than 30 min; anything needing a system setting or elevation.
