## 7. Stop conditions
Stop, record in docs/handoff.md and report; never work around:
- a file whose hash is off its pin, or a launch spec that differs (the user re-pins);
- a launch that fails to join twice in a row, a crash, or the Iris dialog;
- a window's cut rows and its frames differing by more than one at either edge after one fix; timers dropping frames at maxPending 3, or a pass list missing a pipeline stage, after one fix;
- a Chunky task without progress both frozen and unfrozen, or its end read neither in latest.log nor from `chunky progress`; after prep, the tagged mobs moved or the mobs or end crystals not all counted;
- the pinned Iris jar disagreeing with P17; a perf view below 0.9 GPU-bound on the baseline, or a view function failing: the views are the user's;
- no heap under D43 at which full mode holds the twin and one candidate;
- shadows off not detected, or the twin beyond the threshold in two runs of a mode; a mode's geo-mean threshold above 2 % (ALC's per-view thresholds: 0.5-1.3 %);
- two sessions in a row ending invalid on focus, or D42's wait refusing twice (the machine is in use);
- a phase's tests not green within twice its estimate; a cap cut that needs a decision; CI red for a cause outside the repository for more than 30 min; anything needing a system setting or elevation.
