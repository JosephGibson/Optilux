## 1. Goal and exit
- Goal: a shader change is judged by its frames as well as its GPU time: Optilux's hello pack renders the same bits across launches with TAA off, frames are taken at every view and compared by tier, offline checks gate a build before its launch and the similarity check before its commit, and both perf modes are calibrated on the static-texture set every session now uses.
- Exit (design.md#6-milestones M3; roadmap.md#m1-to-m6), all of:
  - `verify similarity`, `check L0|L1|L2`, `run`'s visual and dump kinds, `compare`'s tiers and the review page run, tests green; the pre-commit hook runs the similarity check on staged shader changes;
  - the hello pack with BENCH_DETERMINISTIC and config/pipeline.json v0 passes `check L0` and `verify similarity`; the iso profile v1 passes L0's profile check;
  - the static-textures pack holds every animated texture on its first frame, in the deterministic set every session uses;
  - bench_263's coverage views built, the snapshot retaken and hashed;
  - two visual sessions of the hello pack on full mode's 11 perf views: every view identical (TAA off);
  - A5 judged over 33 waits (D68);
  - config/calibrations/ holds a quick and a full calibration on the deterministic set; per mode a run whose twin lies inside the geo-mean threshold and whose shadows-off candidate is faster beyond it;
  - CI green on every push and on the PR; the user's rebase merge and release v0.4.0; docs/handoff.md written.

## 2. Scope
- In: `verify similarity`, `check L0|L1|L2`; `run`'s visual and dump kinds and A5; `compare`'s tiers, the review page; `install`'s glslang, RGA, reference copies and static-textures pack; the hello pack, pipeline.json v0, iso profile v1; the mod's aligned, ready-triggered and image-less captures; coverage views in `world prep`; the deterministic set in every session; the recalibration; F17 and F25-F27.

## 7. Stop conditions
Stop, record in docs/handoff.md and report; never work around:
- a file whose hash is off its pin, or a launch spec that differs (the user re-pins);
- a launch that fails to join twice in a row, a crash, or the Iris dialog;
- the hello pack failing to load (a compile error or a fallback) after one fix;
- a span from reference/ that a rewrite cannot clear, or controls that need the similarity rule changed;
- an aligned frame off its phase, or a ready-triggered frame not the first after the predicate, after one fix;
- a perf view not identical across the two sessions, or a capture pair moving at a perf view, after one fix (to diagnose: entities' animation, the stepped thunder, the End, readiness); a coverage item missing from its frames, the campfire's particles aside;
- in a calibration: shadows off not detected, or the twin beyond the threshold in two runs of a mode; a geo-mean threshold above 2 %; two sessions in a row invalid on focus, or D42 refusing twice; a fault in a measured module (a fix voids the sessions run);
- a phase's tests not green within twice its estimate; a cap cut that needs a decision; CI red for a cause outside the repository for more than 30 min; anything needing a system setting or elevation.
